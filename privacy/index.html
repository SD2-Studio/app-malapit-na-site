Overview

Malapit Na is a destination-alarm app for Android. You choose a stop and the app uses your device's location to alert you as you approach it. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, Fri Vei As analytics, or Fri Vei As backend.

Location and ride data

When you start a destination alarm, Malapit Na processes precise or approximate location on your device to calculate the distance to your chosen stop. With your permission, this continues when the screen is off or the app is closed or not in use. A visible ongoing notification is shown while the ride is active. Location processing stops when you cancel the ride or dismiss the alarm.

Live location fixes are held temporarily in memory and are not added to ride history or sent to Fri Vei As. A system geofence may be registered on the device as a backup alarm trigger.

Data stored on your device

Malapit Na stores saved-stop names and coordinates, an active destination and alarm distance, ride history, language and alarm settings, the last map view, and any offline map packs you download on your device. This app-controlled data is not uploaded to Fri Vei As.

Delete individual saved stops in the app. Delete downloaded map packs in Settings → Offline maps. Cancel a ride to remove the active destination. Clearing Malapit Na's app data or uninstalling the app removes all app-controlled stored data. There is no remote account record for Fri Vei As to delete.

Maps and network requests

The map is drawn on your device by MapLibre, an open-source map library, using map tiles from OpenFreeMap, a free public tile service. While the map is on screen, your phone requests the tiles it needs from tiles.openfreemap.org over HTTPS. There is no map API key, no map account, and no map SDK telemetry, and the requests carry no advertising identifier and no identifier created by this app.

Like any request your phone makes to a website, a tile request tells that server your IP address and standard connection metadata, along with which tiles were asked for—which indicates, roughly, the part of the map you are looking at. Nothing in the request identifies you, and there is no account for it to be attached to.

Map fonts and icons are bundled inside the app and are never downloaded, so the map keeps its labels even with no connection.

You can download an offline map pack for your area or your route in Settings → Offline maps. Those tiles come from the same tile service and are then stored in Malapit Na's private storage on your phone. The pack, its name, and the date you created it stay on the device; nothing about a pack is uploaded. Delete a pack in Offline maps, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app to remove it.

If you explicitly share a Google Maps short link with Malapit Na, the app sends a limited request to Google to follow that link and read coordinates from its destination. Google necessarily receives the request's IP address and standard connection metadata. The shared link is not retained by Malapit Na after it is resolved.

Google processes that short-link request under its own terms and privacy policy; see Google's privacy policy. OpenFreeMap is an independent public tile service, operated by neither Fri Vei As nor Google; see openfreemap.org.

Permissions

  • Precise, approximate, and background location: calculate distance and keep an active destination alarm working when the app is not in use.
  • Notifications and full-screen alarms: show ride progress and alert you over the lock screen.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, vibration, and boot: keep an armed ride visible, sound the alarm, and restore a recent active ride after a device restart.
  • Internet: load map tiles, download an offline map pack when you ask for one, and resolve a Google Maps link you explicitly share.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state: check whether you are online so the app can explain a blank map. The Wi-Fi state permission is contributed by the map library; the app does not read your Wi-Fi networks.

You can change permissions in Android Settings. Removing location permission prevents the destination alarm from tracking your ride.

Children

Malapit Na is not designed for children under 13. Fri Vei As does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or backend.

Security, changes, and contact

App-controlled ride data remains in Android's private app storage. Malapit Na does not operate a server that receives this data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and device security also depends on your Android settings and physical control of the device.

We may update this policy when app behavior, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The public policy will show a new effective date. For privacy questions, email support@madeirabus.com.

Buod

Ang Malapit Na ay destination-alarm app para sa Android. Pumipili ka ng babaan at ginagamit ng app ang lokasyon ng device mo para alertuhan ka kapag malapit ka na. Walang account system, advertising SDK, Fri Vei As analytics, o Fri Vei As backend ang app.

Lokasyon at datos ng biyahe

Kapag nagsimula ka ng destination alarm, pinoproseso ng Malapit Na sa device mo ang precise o approximate location para kalkulahin ang layo sa napili mong babaan. Kapag pinayagan mo, nagpapatuloy ito kahit patay ang screen o sarado o hindi ginagamit ang app. May nakikitang tuloy-tuloy na notification habang aktibo ang biyahe. Hihinto ang pagproseso ng lokasyon kapag kinansela mo ang biyahe o dinismiss ang alarm.

Pansamantalang nasa memory lang ang live location fixes. Hindi ito idinadagdag sa ride history o ipinapadala sa Fri Vei As. Maaaring magrehistro ang app ng system geofence sa device bilang backup na trigger ng alarm.

Datos na naka-store sa device

Naka-store sa device mo ang pangalan at coordinates ng mga saved na babaan, aktibong destination at alarm distance, ride history, language at alarm settings, huling map view, at ang mga offline map pack na dina-download mo. Hindi ina-upload sa Fri Vei As ang datos na ito.

Maaari mong burahin ang bawat saved na babaan sa app. Burahin ang mga na-download na map pack sa Mga setting → Offline na mapa. Kanselahin ang biyahe para alisin ang aktibong destination. Kapag nag-clear ka ng app data o in-uninstall ang Malapit Na, mabubura ang lahat ng datos na kontrolado ng app. Walang remote account record na kailangang burahin ng Fri Vei As.

Mapa at network requests

Ang mapa ay iginuguhit sa device mo ng MapLibre, isang open-source na map library, gamit ang map tiles mula sa OpenFreeMap, isang libreng pampublikong tile service. Habang nasa screen ang mapa, hinihingi ng phone mo ang mga tile na kailangan nito sa tiles.openfreemap.org sa pamamagitan ng HTTPS. Walang map API key, walang map account, at walang map SDK telemetry, at walang advertising identifier o anumang identifier na gawa ng app na kasama sa mga request na ito.

Tulad ng kahit anong request ng phone mo sa isang website, sinasabi ng tile request sa server na iyon ang IP address mo at ang karaniwang connection metadata, pati kung aling mga tile ang hiningi—na tantiyang nagpapakita kung saang bahagi ng mapa ka nakatingin. Walang nasa request na nagpapakilala sa iyo, at walang account na maiuugnay dito.

Nakabalot na sa loob ng app ang mga font at icon ng mapa at hindi na ito dina-download, kaya may mga label pa rin ang mapa kahit walang koneksyon.

Maaari kang mag-download ng offline map pack para sa lugar mo o sa ruta mo sa Mga setting → Offline na mapa. Galing sa parehong tile service ang mga tile na iyon at ini-store ang mga ito sa private storage ng Malapit Na sa phone mo. Nananatili sa device ang pack, ang pangalan nito, at ang petsa ng paggawa mo rito; walang ini-upload tungkol sa isang pack. Burahin ang pack sa Offline na mapa, i-clear ang app data, o i-uninstall ang app para maalis ito.

Kung ikaw mismo ang mag-share ng Google Maps short link sa Malapit Na, magpapadala ang app ng limitadong request sa Google para sundan ang link at basahin ang coordinates. Matatanggap ng Google ang IP address at karaniwang connection metadata ng request. Hindi itinatago ng Malapit Na ang shared link pagkatapos itong ma-resolve.

Pinoproseso ng Google ang request na iyon para sa short link ayon sa sarili nitong terms at privacy policy; tingnan ang privacy policy ng Google. Ang OpenFreeMap ay isang malayang pampublikong tile service na hindi pinapatakbo ng Fri Vei As o ng Google; tingnan ang openfreemap.org.

Mga permission

  • Precise, approximate, at background location: kalkulahin ang layo at panatilihing gumagana ang aktibong destination alarm kapag hindi ginagamit ang app.
  • Notifications at full-screen alarms: ipakita ang progreso ng biyahe at ang alarm sa ibabaw ng lock screen.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, vibration, at boot: panatilihing nakikita ang aktibong biyahe, patunugin ang alarm, at ibalik ang bagong aktibong biyahe pagkatapos mag-restart ang device.
  • Internet: mag-load ng map tiles, mag-download ng offline map pack kapag ikaw ang humiling, at mag-resolve ng Google Maps link na ikaw mismo ang nag-share.
  • Network at Wi-Fi state: tingnan kung online ka para maipaliwanag ng app ang blangkong mapa. Galing sa map library ang Wi-Fi state permission; hindi binabasa ng app ang mga Wi-Fi network mo.

Maaari mong baguhin ang mga permission sa Android Settings. Kapag inalis ang location permission, hindi matatrack ng destination alarm ang biyahe mo.

Mga bata

Hindi ginawa ang Malapit Na para sa mga batang wala pang 13 taong gulang. Hindi sinasadyang mangolekta ng Fri Vei As ng personal na impormasyon mula sa mga bata sa pamamagitan ng account o backend.

Seguridad, mga pagbabago, at contact

Nananatili sa private app storage ng Android ang app-controlled ride data. Walang server ang Malapit Na na tumatanggap ng datos na ito. Walang paraan ng storage o transmission na ganap na ligtas, at nakadepende rin ang seguridad sa Android settings at pisikal na kontrol mo sa device.

Maaari naming i-update ang policy kapag nagbago ang app behavior, SDKs, o legal requirements. Ipapakita sa public policy ang bagong effective date. Para sa mga tanong tungkol sa privacy, mag-email sa support@madeirabus.com.
